Project 01

Viking xprs Helsinki-Tallinn

This course was installed on the Viking XPRS ferry between Helsinki and Tallinn. The course is a 9 hole Mini Wood course. The course is designed to be fun for all ages and skill levels.

Project 02

Wanhat wehkeet, Karstula, Finland

This 18 hole course is located in Finland, near Karstula in a tourism and leisure center Wanhat Wehkeet.

Project 03

Tykkimäki Resort, Kouvola, Finland

This 12 hole wood park course is located at the Tykkimäki Resort in Kouvola, Finland.

Project 04

Taste Day & night, Porvoo, Finland

This 9 hole wood park course is located in Porvoo, Finland. It's in a restaurant called Taste Day & night.
